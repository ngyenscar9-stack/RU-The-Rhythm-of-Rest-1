Ru

the rhythm of rest

Ru is a Vietnamese word for the gentle act of lulling someone to rest — through rhythm, sound and quiet comfort. At voco Scenia Bay Nha Trang, we reimagine that familiar idea as something personal: a room prepared around the way you rest.

Choose the pillow that suits you

Soft, firm or cool — choose the feeling you would like to take into sleep.

Choose a scent of the coast

Three notes drawn from Khánh Hòa, placed lightly in the room with a small card telling you where each one begins.

What would you like to hear tonight?

A sound kept very low to close a long day. It softens and fades after ninety minutes.

Make the room your own

Before you return to your room, the curtains are drawn and the temperature set to your choosing.

The bay lies to the east. Mornings at Scenia Bay begin with the first light.
